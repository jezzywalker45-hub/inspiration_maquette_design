What is sound therapy?

Sound therapy is a holistic wellness practice that uses sound vibrations and frequencies to support relaxation, emotional balance, and energetic alignment.

Using instruments such as Tibetan singing bowls, tuning forks, crystal sound tools, and voice, sound therapy allows the body and mind to slow down and return to a state of balance naturally.

Sound vibrations may help to:

  • release physical and mental tension
  • reduce stress and anxiety
  • improve sleep quality
  • rebalance the energy centers (chakras)
  • induce deep relaxation and inner harmony
